## Scene 1: “A New Dawn”
- Player’s **18th birthday**; celebration with parents
- Side depends on which bank they live on
- Gestapo (East) or Tokko (West) arrives
- Adult → must choose:
  1. **Join agency** (help quell resistance) → report to HQ next morning → **Scene 2A**
  2. **Stay with parents** → farm (JP / West) or factory (DE / East) → **Scene 2B**

---

## Scene 2A: Agency Life
- Report to agency HQ → **large** reputation bump for overlord faction
- Escorted to field officer = **handler** (Otto Hollen East / Takeshi Kurohane West)
- Job: infiltrate resistance, report findings
- Bait intel:
  - **West (JP):** location of **nitrate** shipment (bombs)
  - **East (DE):** location of **weapons/ammo** shipment
- Sent to resistance hangout (bar / supply store)
- **Mitchell Connor** recognizes player (“you’re so-and-so’s kid… just turn 18?”)
- Offers vague “job” → player can infer resistance work
- **Choice:** come clean to Connor (agency) **or** play along (double agent)
  - Covert / play along → **Scene 3A → 3B / 3B2**
  - Disclose → **Scene 3B3**

---

## Scene 2B: Work Life
- Work job → time passes → money + **slight** reputation for all factions on that side of river
- Caught in middle of resistance smuggling → hauled to Gestapo/Tokko HQ
- Same field officer questions them; offers agency join under **threat of death** (player + parents)
- Forces into **Scene 2A**, with modified Connor line: “I heard you got wrapped up in the raid the other day”
- Same disclose vs double-agent choice

---

## Scene 3A: Stay covert (setup)
- Player accepted Connor’s job without disclosing
- Connor sets meet location (side-dependent) → **3B** (West) or **3B2** (East)

### Scene 3B — West (Japanese): riverbank farm meet
1. Meet at farm, north end, river border
2. Tractor man → points to riverbank
3. Connor + fighters waiting; package arriving; load horse-drawn carriage to the west
4. First: retrieve package **from** carriage = **bag of nitrate** → return to bluff
5. Connor gone; commotion across water at dock warehouse
6. Fighters: Connor moved upriver for a better look
7. Japanese patrol boat + soldiers from fields → surrender; one flees to water → shot by boat
8. Marched to farm → **Takeshi Kurohane** present → “take them away”
9. Fighters split to separate holding areas
10. Player taken to hut near agency HQ → Takeshi praises “leading” men to fighters → released → report HQ for debrief
11. *(Setup: player used as bait; raid success for Tokko)*

### Scene 3B2 — East (German): warehouse / dock meet
1. Meet warehouse + dock, north shore, opposite the JP farm
2. Worker unloading truck → “Connor sent me” → help unload into warehouse
3. Player realizes cargo = **ammo + gun crates**
4. Inside: Connor thanks them, leaves in truck
5. After 2nd package: vehicles, “hands up” DE/EN → setup
6. **Otto Hollen** + ~10 soldiers; workers apprehended
7. Otto: great work finding storage; they followed player
8. Prisoners marched away *(later lore: traded to JP side as laborers — player unaware)*
9. Otto: report HQ for debrief

---

## Scene 3B3: Disclose to resistance
- Connor suspicious: “How do I know I can trust you?”
- Player options:
  1. “You’ll just have to trust me” → Connor rejects (family reputation not enough)
  2. *(Flow as written continues to reveal shipment location)*
- Player gives shipment location (nitrate or weapons); explains handler told them to use it to gain trust
- Connor: skeptical (“sounds like that’s what you’re trying to do”)
- Player: doesn’t want to work for occupier; wants nation free
- Connor accepts → **retrieve shipment and bring it to us**
