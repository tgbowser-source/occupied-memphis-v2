## East footprint (Memphis) — bounds

- **West edge:** Mississippi River / cobble front + Wolf River Harbor shoreline  
- **North:** Wolf River Harbor / Uptown industrial shore (Ch1 dock zone)  
- **South:** South Bluffs warehouse district + Harahan / Frisco bridge approaches  
- **East edge:** about **Third–Beale corridor** a few blocks inland (CBD density); do not stretch to Midtown

### East districts (in play)
1. **CBD / Sterick** — Madison & N. Third  
2. **Cotton Row / Front** — Front between Monroe–Gayoso  
3. **Beale corridor** — hangouts + A. Schwab  
4. **Wolf Harbor / Uptown** — Ch1 warehouse/dock  
5. **South Bluffs** — secondary warehouses / rail  
6. **Bridge approach** — Harahan east landing

## West footprint (West Memphis) — bounds

- **East edge:** river + St. Francis Levee + Harahan approach viaduct  
- **Core:** Broadway (US 70) × Missouri (US 61) + City Hall (100 Court St)  
- **North:** leveeside farmland toward Mound City corridor (Ch1 farm)  
- **South / west:** highway strip + light industrial (pull street names from 1949 Sanborn later; don’t invent)

### West districts (in play)
1. **Downtown WM** — Broadway × Missouri  
2. **City Hall / Tokko–Kempeitai HQ** — 100 Court St  
3. **8th Street nightlife** (“Beale Street West”)  
4. **North leveeside farms** — Ch1 meet  
5. **Bridge approach viaduct** — long bottomland approach to Harahan
