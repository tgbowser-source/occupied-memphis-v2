## Historical notes (research)
See `research/05-memphis-1945-1950-map.md`.
- Real crossings 1945–50: Frisco (rail), Harahan (rail + wagonways), Memphis & Arkansas Hwy (opens Dec 1949). Fiction: the 1949 war halts the M&A highway bridge before it opens, so Harahan stays the only road crossing.
- Sterick: Madison & N. Third, 1928–29 / opened 1930.
- West Memphis City Hall: 100 Court St, **1939** (not 1930).
