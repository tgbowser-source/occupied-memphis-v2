**Rule:** Optional content **Chapter 2+ only** — unlocks **after** Chapter 1 spine resolves (birthday → agency/work → Connor → raid/disclose). **No overlap** with Ch1 concurrent beats. # Side Quest 1: “The Ledger Next Door” — Moral choice (East primary; West mirror)

**Bank:** East start preferred (H1 / Beale). West mirror uses Mae Bedford + Tokko flavor (see Scene 2W).  
**Tone:** Moral choice — protect a neighbor vs buy safety with someone else’s names.  
**When:** **Chapter 2+** only (after Ch1 Connor / raid / disclose resolves). Works on work path or agency path; handler may notice papers. Player already has Ch1 outcome flags (resistance contact / agency standing / disclose lean).

---

## Side Quest: The Ledger Next Door — Scene 1

**Unlock:** Chapter 2 opens. Weeks (or more) after the Ch1 raid/disclose — the birthday cake is long gone; the house has settled into whatever post-Ch1 normal the player earned.

Evening at **H1**. Across the fence, **Ruth Ellison** knocks soft — three taps, pause, two — the old neighborhood code from before the occupation learned how to listen.

Ruth’s coat is threadbare wool, 1942 cut, black armband still pinned wrong. She presses a thin cloth-bound ledger into the player’s hands. Inside: dates, street names, who slept in whose attic when the first Wehrmacht trucks rolled Madison in ’45. “Gestapo came this afternoon,” she whispers. “They want *names*. I won’t give them yours. I won’t give them anyone’s. Hide this. Or burn it. Just — don’t let Fleischer’s boys find it on my table.”

A street patrol passes the corner — Wehrmacht greatcoats, lamps. Ruth’s porch light is already dark.

*(If Ch1 left the player hot with Gestapo/Tokko heat, Ruth’s knock lands harder — she may say she waited until “things quieted.” Do not restage Ch1’s Connor meet or raid.)*

**Player choices:**
1. **Take the ledger** and hide it at H1 (attic / under floor / mother’s sewing chest).
2. **Refuse** — tell Ruth to burn it herself; you won’t risk the family.
3. **Offer to walk it to R1 (A. Schwab)** tonight and ask Connor’s people what to do with “history that can hang people.”
4. *(Agency path only)* **Promise to “handle it”** while already calculating Otto’s interest.

---

### If player chooses 1 (hide at H1) → Scene 2A

Morning. Mother finds the ledger while putting away linens. Quiet confrontation: she understands; she is terrified. Father says nothing at breakfast, then: “If they come for her, they come through us.”

**Branch:**
- **Keep hiding it** → Scene 3A (search risk).
- **Give it back to Ruth at dusk** → Scene 3B (Ruth moves it; player clean but Ruth more exposed).
- **Burn it in the backyard stove** → Scene 3C (moral relief; later Ruth is taken anyway — empty house; small RES-E rumor: “Smiths burned the only map of who helped”).

**Consequences (keep):**
- Slight **RES-E +** if Ruth survives the week without a knock.
- If Gestapo random-check hits H1 while ledger is present: detain risk, **GER −**, possible coercion echo of Scene 2B (do not force Ch1 rewrite — soft fail → fine in US Marks + Residence Card stamp “reviewed”).
- Family trust flag for later quests.

---

### If player chooses 2 (refuse) → Scene 2B

Ruth nods once, eyes flat. That night the ledger burns in her stove — player can see the glow from H1. Two days later Ruth’s house is empty. Neighbors say “questioning at Sterick.” No body, no notice.

**Consequences:**
- **GER** unchanged / slight + if player later mentions “I told her to cooperate” at a checkpoint (optional dialogue).
- **RES-E −** (cold whisper: Smith kid wouldn’t dirty hands).
- Hook: Connor at R1 later: “Ellison’s gone. You know anything about a book?”

---

### If player chooses 3 (take to R1) → Scene 2C

**R1 — A. Schwab.** Daylight safer. Player browses like a civilian; slips word to the counter that “a friend of Connor’s friend has paper trouble.” A runner (not Connor if Ch1 fork not ready — use Cal Pike if Connor unavailable) takes the ledger into the back.

**If Connor relationship exists (post–Ch1 fork, Ch2+):** Connor glances, does not smile. “That’s not cargo. That’s a noose.” He offers:
- Archive it off-site (player earns **RES-E +**, small US Marks stipend later).
- Use select pages to warn people still on the list (**RES-E ++**, increases Gestapo heat on Beale for 48 hours — soft checkpoint frequency up).

**If Connor unavailable / burned / not in contact (Ch1 path variant):** Cal Pike takes custody for “the right pocket.” Player gets a Resistance Token passphrase for later. **RES-E** slight +.

---

### If player chooses 4 (agency path — “handle it”) → Scene 2D

Player reports a “civilian document of interest” to **Otto Hollen** without naming Ruth first — or names her.

**If vague report:** Otto assigns surveillance on the block. Player must plant a listening excuse (borrowed sugar, returned tool). **GER +**. Ruth still free for now → Scene 3A risk rises.

**If names Ruth:** Otto sends a pickup. Ruth disappears by nightfall. **GER ++**, **RES-E −−**. Mother will not look at the player for a day. Otto: “Good instinct. Don’t grow a conscience on my time.”

---

## Side Quest: The Ledger Next Door — Scene 3A (search)

Knock at H1 after curfew exception window — Gestapo spot check. Papers out. If ledger still home:

**Player choices:**
1. Stall at the door while a parent moves the book (timing minigame / skill check — design: success = clear; fail = found).
2. Offer a bribe in **US Marks** (amount TBD; needs cash on hand).
3. Claim agency business (**Agency Provisional** present) — Otto’s name as shield; works only if GER standing Warm+; else “we’ll verify at Sterick.”

**Outcomes:** Found → Scene 2D coercion without reward. Not found → **GER** slight − (wasted check), **family** relief, Ruth owes a favor (hook).

---

## Side Quest: The Ledger Next Door — Scene 2W (West mirror)

At **H2**, **Mae Bedford** brings a different “ledger”: a kitchen composition book of who still has insulin and who the Tokko clinic cut off. Same moral skeleton — hide / refuse / take to R3 or Connor / report to **Takeshi**. Swap Gestapo↔Tokko, US Marks↔SDB notes, Sterick↔City Hall. Ruth’s role = Mae’s neighbor network; do not claim Mae *is* Swims.

---

### Hooks / later
- Ruth (or Mae) returns as a witness who can clear or damn the player in a mid-game tribunal scene.
- Burned-ledger path: resistance asks player to reconstruct names from memory (unreliable narrator quest).
- Agency path who sold Ruth: Imperialist rumor — Stauffenberg’s circle mutters that Gestapo “farms children as informants.” Soft **IMP-E** curiosity flag only.

# Side Quest 2: “Canvas and Cottonseed” — Smuggling (both banks, side-dependent)

**Bank:** Both — East uses **D1**; West uses **F1**; optional seam via river rumor (not required to cross BR).  
**Tone:** Smuggling under work cover.  
**When:** **Chapter 2+** only. Player already holds Work Pass and/or Agency night auth from Ch1. Optional job from Connor *or* from Spoon/Mae — different cargo and crew than Ch1 shipment; do not restage nitrate/ammo raid beats.

---

## Side Quest: Canvas and Cottonseed — Scene 1

**Unlock:** Chapter 2+. Harbor/farm work has gone routine again after Ch1’s chaos; this is a *new* quiet job, not a shadow of the Ch1 shipment.

A note finds the player — left in a lunch pail at **W1** (East) or under a tractor seat at **W2/F1** (West):

> Need a quiet back. Canvas rolls. Not guns. Pay on delivery. — C

If Connor relationship exists post–Ch1, the initial is real. If Connor is burned, absent, or cold, **Tom “Spoon” Reilly** (East) or **Mae Bedford** (West) claims the note when asked — “Connor’s busy; I’m the hands.”

**Job brief:** Move three canvas-wrapped bundles labeled “cottonseed meal” from a legitimate warehouse stack to a civilian cart. Inside: radio tubes / crystal sets / printing ink (resistance press parts) — **not** Ch1 nitrate/ammo; **not** a redo of the Ch1 raid. Keep grounded.

**Player choices:**
1. **Take the job.**
2. **Walk away.**
3. *(Agency)* **Report the note** to Otto/Takeshi before accepting.

---

### If player walks away → end soft
Spoon/Mae: “Smart. Or yellow. We’ll know which.” Slight **RES** − on that bank. Hook remains available once.

---

### If player reports to handler → Scene 1H

**Otto (East):** Wants the drop watched, not busted mid-load — “I want the next link.” Player must still move the canvas as bait. Thematically adjacent to Ch1 pressure but **different cargo and NPCs** (Spoon’s crew, not Connor’s Ch1 fighters) — treat as Ch2 work, not a flashback. **GER +**. Risk: if Spoon learns, D1 access closes.

**Takeshi (West):** Same structure at F1 leveeside; IJA patrol timing. **JPN +**.

→ Then Scene 2 with flag `handler_watching`.

---

## Side Quest: Canvas and Cottonseed — Scene 2 (load)

### If on German side (East / D1)

Dusk at Wolf Harbor warehouse apron. Spoon unlocks a side door. Harbor smell: creosote, river mud, cigarette. Bundles are heavier than seed. A Wehrmacht truck idles two piers down — not for you, yet.

**Player choices while loading:**
1. **Load clean and fast** — leave.
2. **Open one bundle** to confirm contents (tubes/ink) — knowledge flag; Spoon angry if caught looking.
3. **Palm one tube** for later sale/Cal Pike (**black-market hook**).
4. If `handler_watching`: **delay** so Otto’s men see the cart leave (success = GER ++; Spoon may still escape).

Cart destination: alley behind Beale near R1 — not into Schwab’s front door.

### If on Japanese side (West / F1)

Late afternoon, cotton tract. Mae points to a horse cart already half-loaded with real seed. “Bottom layer’s fake. Top layer’s honest. Patrols smell honest.” IJA bicycle patrol on the levee road.

**Player choices:**
1. Drive the cart west toward Broadway fringe drop.
2. Take the long bottomland path (slower, fewer eyes).
3. If `handler_watching`: “forget” a tarp so Tokko can track dust — Takeshi pleased; Mae’s kitchen may burn later (hook).

---

## Side Quest: Canvas and Cottonseed — Scene 3 (delivery heat)

A soft checkpoint appears — not Harahan; a street/levee stop.

**Papers:** Residence Card + Work Pass. If night: need curfew exception or fail → detain.

**If player stole a tube:** search chance up; if found, “theft from military salvage” fiction — fine in bank currency + **overlord −**.

**If clean delivery:**
- Pay: **US Marks** (East) or **SDB notes** (West), plus optional hard good (coffee tin / soap).
- **RES +** on that bank.
- Connor (if available) later: “You move quiet. Remember that.”

**If handler bait succeeded:**
- Overlord **++**; resistance contact (Spoon/Mae) goes to ground.
- Player may be asked to “find them again” — later hook, not Ch1 rewrite.

**If player warns Spoon/Mae about handler bait mid-job:**
- Delivery aborts; **RES ++**, **overlord −** if handler learns; Connor trust warm for disclose-leaning players.

---

### Hooks / later
- Palm’d radio tube becomes a crafting/repair key for a resistance listening post.
- Spoon can introduce Boss’s name as rumor only (“Boss doesn’t meet kids”).
- Failed bait: Otto/Takeshi assign a punishment detail — paperwork duty at HQ — comedy-tension scene.

# Side Quest 3: “Mother’s Clinic Hour” — Family (both; home-bank)

**Bank:** Both (H1+Hana Okada via rumor / H2+Hana local).  
**Tone:** Family pressure under occupation medicine scarcity.  
**When:** **Chapter 2+** only. Especially potent if parents remain central (work path) or if Ch1 agency lean left the player cold to family — use post–Ch1 relationship flags; do not restage birthday/home Scene 1.

---

## Side Quest: Mother’s Clinic Hour — Scene 1

**Unlock:** Chapter 2+. Home life has absorbed whatever Ch1 cost the family; this illness is a *new* crisis, not concurrent with the birthday or first raid night.

At home (**H1** or **H2**), Mother is fever-bright, pretending she isn’t. Father finally says it: the occupation clinic stamped **DENIED** on her sulfa request — “nonessential civilian.” A neighbor whispers **Hana Okada** on the West bank still has vials… for people who can pay in quiet.

**Problem:** Hana’s clinic sits on **8th Street**, West.  
- **West-start players:** reachable same bank; curfew and Tokko eyes.  
- **East-start players:** need **Crossing Permit** + Harahan (**BR**) or a smuggler boat favor (expensive, RES-gated) — or find an East substitute (design: Josef Lange knows a Sterick infirmary orderly who steals veterinary sulfa — worse quality).

**Player choices:**
1. **Go through legal clinic appeal** (queue theater).
2. **Seek Hana** (West) — cross if needed.
3. **Seek Lange’s veterinary route** (East-only alternate).
4. **Ask Connor / resistance** for medical favor.
5. **Ask handler** (Otto/Takeshi) for a “family compassion” chit — costs reputation debt.

---

## Side Quest: Mother’s Clinic Hour — Scene 2A (legal appeal)

Hours in a wooden chair under a portrait of the local overlord. Clerk speaks slow English. Appeal denied unless player has Warm+ overlord standing **or** pays a “processing gift.”

**Pay:** steep **US Marks** / **SDB notes**.  
**Success:** official sulfa, no criminal flag. Mother recovers. Family grateful. Soft **overlord +** (you used their system).  
**Fail:** lost day of work (W1/W2 wage miss → money −, employer warning).

---

## Side Quest: Mother’s Clinic Hour — Scene 2B (Hana / West)

### If already on West

Night approach to Hana’s back door. Midwife apron, hair pinned 1940s neat. She names a price in **SDB notes** — or a favor: deliver a sealed letter to Mae Bedford (no open).

**Player choices:**
1. Pay cash.
2. Do the letter favor (links Quest 1/2 networks).
3. Threaten / flash agency papers — Hana goes cold; may sell diluted meds; **JPN** heat if Tokko later raids and finds your name in her book.

### If on East — must reach West

→ **Scene 2B-BR** (checkpoint mini-quest embedded):

**Harahan Bridge queue.** Lila Vargas slides along the rail: “You look like a kid with a sick story. I sell better stories.” She offers a script: “farm tools for uncle” vs “medical errand” vs “agency courier.”

**Player choices at BR:**
1. Honest medical reason — search depth depends on GER/JPN standing; meds for Mother may be confiscated “for inspection.”
2. Buy Lila’s script (small currency) — lower search if performance passes.
3. Use Crossing Permit if owned.
4. Turn back; take Lange route instead.

Crossing success → Hana scene as above. Confiscation → Mother worsens; urgency flag for Scene 3.

---

## Side Quest: Mother’s Clinic Hour — Scene 2C (Lange / East vet sulfa)

**Josef Lange** meets the player near a Sterick service alley — not inside HQ. He wants something soft: a rumor. “They say Von Stauffenberg told a captain the Gestapo should stop breaking pianists’ hands. You hear talk like that on Beale?”

**Player choices:**
1. Admit you’ve heard Imperialist whispers (true or bluff) → Lange warms; sells vet sulfa cheap; soft **IMP-E +** seed.
2. Deny all politics → higher price.
3. Report Lange’s questions to Otto later → **GER +**, Lange disappears (hook: Imperialist cautionary tale).

Meds work slower/risk subplot (mother sick longer) — keep grounded, not cartoon poison.

---

## Side Quest: Mother’s Clinic Hour — Scene 2D (Connor / handler)

**Connor:** “We don’t run a hospital. We run a future.” Still — he can point to Hana or a Beale pharmacist if **RES** Warm. Cost: one future favor flag `owes_connor_medical`.

**Handler:** Issues a one-time clinic voucher. **Overlord +**. Parents relieved but uneasy (“you’re their boy now”). If double-agent, resistance may later ask why Mother got Reich/Empire medicine so fast.

---

## Side Quest: Mother’s Clinic Hour — Scene 3 (aftermath)

Mother recovers enough to stand in the kitchen. She asks — gently — what the player paid that isn’t money.

**Consequences (record flags):**
- `family_trust++` if player avoided selling neighbors / Lange.
- `debt_connor` / `debt_handler` / `debt_hana_letter`.
- Currency drain as paid.
- If BR confiscation happened: Crossing Permit harder next time (admin flag).

---

### Hooks / later
- Hana’s letter contains a name Minamoto’s circle would care about (anti-Tokko excess) — opens Imperialist whisper questline without forcing it.
- Father may volunteer the player for a “safe” factory overtime that is actually a Boss/Swims lookout — family unknowingly recruiting.

# Side Quest 4: “Fair Rate / False Rate” — Checkpoint + Imperialist whisper + black-market exchange

**Bank:** Designed for **both** with side-dependent desks and changers.  
**Tone:** Black-market exchange under checkpoint pressure; Imperialist splinter as texture (not open recruitment).  
**When:** **Chapter 2+** only. Ideal once the player holds mismatched currency from Ch2 travel/pay/bribes/smuggling — or after a post–Ch1 Harahan errand. Do not gate on Ch1 birthday or first Connor meet.

---

## Side Quest: Fair Rate / False Rate — Scene 1

**Unlock:** Chapter 2+. Currency friction after the player is already moving between banks/jobs as a post–Ch1 operative or worker — not a first-week learning beat.

Player holds the “wrong” paper money for where they stand:
- On East with **SDB notes**, or
- On West with **US Marks**, or
- A mixed purse after a favor.

Official advice: use the **occupation exchange desk** near HQ / bridge admin. Street advice (Lila / Cal): desks skin you unless the overlord smiles at your Residence Card.

**Player choices:**
1. Go to **official desk**.
2. Go to **black-market changer** (**Cal Pike** East alley / West 8th back room via Mae intro).
3. Try to spend foreign scrip raw at a shop (usually refused; sometimes reported).

---

## Side Quest: Fair Rate / False Rate — Scene 2A (official desk)

Fluorescent flicker, bilingual forms, armed bored soldier at the door.

Clerk checks reputation under the table:
- **Hostile/unknown:** refused or extreme haircut; optional “secondary screening” → checkpoint strip-search; if smuggling leftovers in inventory, arrest threat.
- **Neutral:** bad rate, usable.
- **Favored (agency):** best legal rate + shorter queue.
- **Handler-backed:** near-fair; clerk stamps Crossing Permit application “recommended” (hook).

**Twist — Imperialist whisper:** While waiting, player overhears two officers:

### If on German side
A Wehrmacht lieutenant mutters that **Wilhelm Von Stauffenberg** called Gestapo interrogations “a stain on the uniform.” The other hisses silence. Player may:
1. Ignore.
2. Note it for later (journal flag `imp_e_rumor_strauffenberg`).
3. Report the lieutenant’s words to Gestapo desk → **GER +**, lieutenant punished; soft **IMP-E −** before meter even unlocks; Lange (if met) will not help again.

### If on Japanese side
An IJA clerk whispers **Kiyoshi Minamoto** delayed a Tokko sweep of 8th Street “for optics.” Same choice structure with **Takeshi**/Tokko vs **IMP-W** seed.

**Player choices after exchange:**
1. Take the stamped receipt and leave clean.
2. Ask clerk about Crossing Permit (costs; reputation-gated).
3. Argue rate — can trigger guard attention (curfew clock still running).

---

## Side Quest: Fair Rate / False Rate — Scene 2B (black-market changer)

### East — Cal Pike’s alley
Cal weighs notes like tobacco. Rate tracks **RES-E** standing. If player is known agency (Warm+ GER visible to street): Cal refuses or offers punitive rate “tax for the Sterick smell on you.”

**Player choices:**
1. Accept Cal’s rate.
2. Offer a favor instead of haircut — e.g., warn Cal which night Beale gets a Gestapo walk (requires intel from Otto or from Ledger quest).
3. Rob / threaten Cal (allowed as dark option): short-term cash; **RES-E −−**; Beale hangouts close; Connor cold later.
4. Ask Cal what he knows about “officers who hate the secret police but love the flag” — he snorts: “Imperialists. Pretty word for men who still point rifles at us. Careful chasing pretty words.” Soft lore; no join offer.

### West — Mae’s back room changer
Same math with **RES-W** and **SDB↔USM**. Tokko plainclothes risk if player arrives in agency posture.

---

## Side Quest: Fair Rate / False Rate — Scene 3 (the queue trap)

Leaving either venue, a **soft checkpoint** stops the player — CBD edge (East) or Broadway × Missouri (West). They want to see both the currency and the receipt.

**If official desk receipt matches cash:** clear, possible slight **overlord +**.  
**If black-market cash with no receipt:** “Explain.”  

**Player choices:**
1. Bribe (currency).
2. Agency Provisional flash.
3. Lila Vargas appears with a distraction (costs a pack / favor) — only if player hired her earlier at BR or paid her in Scene 2B-BR of Quest 3.
4. Run (curfew/chase fail state → detain → fine → papers “reviewed”).

**Special double-agent beat:** If player used desk *and* Cal the same day, a watcher may note both — future handler interrogation hook: “Why does your purse smell like Beale and Sterick?”

---

## Side Quest: Fair Rate / False Rate — Scene 4 (optional close — whisper made flesh)

Only if `imp_*_rumor` flag set and player did **not** sell the whispering officer:

A sealed unsigned note reaches H1/H2 or the agency pigeonhole:

> Some of us still believe in order without cruelty. Do not seek us. If you must choose a monster, choose the quieter one. — *a friend of the Bridge*

No meeting. No Stauffenberg/Minamoto on-screen yet — texture only, per locked “rumors not banners.”

**Consequences:**
- Currency adjusted to local usable.
- Reputation shifts per path (desk vs Cal; report vs protect whisper).
- Papers: possible Crossing Permit progress; or “reviewed” stamp friction.
- Hooks into later Imperialist quests without rewriting Ch1.

---

### Hooks / later
- Cal Pike becomes standing black-market vendor unlocked by RES tier.
- Desk “recommended” stamp shortens BR queues in Quest 3 repeats.
- Unsigned Imperialist note catalogued for Cast/CoS as first IMP soft-reveal item.
- If player sold the whisperer: mid-game Imperialist content harder / colder on that bank.
