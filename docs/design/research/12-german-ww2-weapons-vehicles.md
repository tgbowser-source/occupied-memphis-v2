# 12 — German WWII Weapons & Vehicles Catalog (Memphis Hub Research)

**Purpose:** Historically accurate survey of German weapons and vehicles that existed by war’s end (≈1939–1945 tech + late-war/prototype programs that existed by 1945), framed for the Memphis occupation present (~1947–48 alt-history).  
**Rule:** Do not invent weapons. Paper-only concepts that never left the drawing board stay marked **Paper-only**. Prototypes that were built/tested by 1945 may be **fielded** and even in **full production** in the alt-1947/48 occupation force.  
**Status columns:**
- **Wartime status:** Mass / Limited / Prototype / Paper-only (historical 1939–45 reality)
- **Alt-1947/48 prod:** Full production / Expanded / Still scarce / Off-map special (game-world production after years of occupation industry)

**Sources (primary reference set):**

## Design guidance for the hub (cross-ref `11-weapons-vehicles.md`)

1. **Street default ≠ full TO&E.** Tier A dominates civilian-facing scenes; B/C appear when factions escalate.
2. **Alt production is a dial, not a free invent.** If it lacked a physical 1945 prototype/design path → do not add.
3. **River Memphis:** Schwimmwagen, Landwasserschlepper, barge logistics > deep-water navy.
4. **Night:** Vampir + vehicle IR are **gameplay systems**, not Easter eggs.
5. **V-weapons:** Prefer radio news, crater set-dressing, special ops against launch sites over player-portable nukes/rockets.
