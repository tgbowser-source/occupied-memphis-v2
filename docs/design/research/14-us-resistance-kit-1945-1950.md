# US / Resistance Kit — Memphis–West Memphis c.1945–1950 (short)

**Source:** Research bot partial delivery, 2026-09-10  
**Purpose:** Game-design kit for locals, police, Guard leftovers, farms/industry, and black-market/G.I. surplus in the Mid-South. **Not** a full U.S. Army TO&E.  
**Window:** 1945–1950.  
**Usefulness tiers:** Common resistance | Uncommon | Rare | Off-map US military.

**Legal note (period):** Ordinary rifles, shotguns, and revolvers were widely and legally owned. Fully automatic weapons fell under the **1934 National Firearms Act** (registration/tax). Unauthorized machine guns = illegal even as “bring-backs” after mid-1945 theater rules tightened.

**Alt-history note (hub):** Ch1 East “gun crates” can be **occupation-seized / diverted military stock**, which is a different channel than open DCM surplus (Research flags Garands/Carbines as rare on the civilian market until the 1950s).
