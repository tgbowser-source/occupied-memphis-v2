**Project:** Thomas Bowser video-game hub (West Memphis occupation)  
**Scope:** IJA + relevant IJN shore / SNLF / Kempeitai kit that existed by Aug 1945

**Rule:** Do not invent. Wartime facts from documented sources. Alt-1947/48 column is **design fiction** built on real 1945 programs — marked clearly.  
**Companion use:** Pair with `11-weapons-vehicles.md` (game pillars) and German East-bank brief when written.

| Column | Values | Meaning |
