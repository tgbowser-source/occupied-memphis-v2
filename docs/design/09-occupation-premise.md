## One-sentence pitch
After a divergent WWII endgame, the Mississippi at Memphis is a frozen front: **Germany holds the east bank, Japan the west**, with **Harahan Bridge** as the only tolerated civilian/military crossing — a pressure valve both empires police but neither fully trusts the other to own.

---

## Divergent history (useful fiction — not real WWII)

**FACT anchor:** Real 1945 = Allied victory; Memphis never occupied.

**Fiction spine (proposed):**
1. **To 1949:** The war runs longer and ends in 1949 in a **negotiated collapse / partial Axis survival** (exact global map can stay foggy). The U.S. interior is carved into **occupation zones** along major rivers and rail hubs rather than a single conqueror.
2. **Memphis chosen** as a dual-empire logistics knot: cotton, rail, and the Mississippi. Neither Axis partner will cede the whole junction.
3. **1949 armistice line:** thalweg / main channel of the Mississippi through the playable footprint. East bank → **German Occupational Authority**; west bank → **Japanese Occupational Authority**.
4. **Bridge settlement:** Frisco remains **rail-only / heavily militarized** (NPC background; not player road). **Harahan** (rail + wagonways) is designated the **sole joint civilian crossing** under a brittle two-power protocol.
5. **Why no new highway bridge (LOCKED rationale):** Historically the **Memphis & Arkansas Bridge** was authorized pre-war, with construction beginning **12 Sep 1945** and opening **17 Dec 1949**. In this timeline the **invasion / occupation halts the project before it opens**, so the modern 4-lane highway span **never carries traffic**. Harahan’s wagonways remain the only auto crossing — not a hand-wave, a consequence of the occupation.
6. **1949–50:** Sterick Building and West Memphis City Hall are seized as high commands. Local US civil structures hollowed out; collaborationist clerks stamp papers; Gestapo/Tokko run political police; Wehrmacht/IJA hold street muscle; Kempeitai/SS sit at the top of each stack.
7. **Present (Ch1):** Occupation feels **settled but not peaceful** — curfews, scrip (US Marks / SDB notes), quotas, resistance (Boss East / Swims West), and “Imperialist” splinter officers who hate the excesses but not their nation’s flag.

---

## Why two empires share one city
- **Logistics:** Neither can afford the other to monopolize Mississippi freight + cotton.
- **Mutual suspicion:** Joint Harahan protocol = inspection theater; each side’s plainclothes (Gestapo / Tokko) watch the other’s soldiers as much as the locals.
- **Resistance benefit:** Smuggling and double agents thrive in the seam (Connor’s world; F1↔D1 river beats).
