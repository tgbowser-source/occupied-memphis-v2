# Daily-life rules (both banks)

**Status:** PROPOSED — awaiting Thomas lock  
**Context:** Footprint locked (`07-playable-footprint.md`); campaign 1950–55 (Ch1 opens Nov 1950); period-accurate wardrobe default  
**Purpose:** What civilians (and the player) can do hour-to-hour without starting a mission

---

## Design pillars
1. **Two occupations, one river** — same pressure, different flavor (German bureaucracy vs Japanese military-police presence).
2. **Harahan is the valve** — crossing is rare, watched, and narratively expensive.
3. **Work is cover** — farm/factory shifts explain presence and income; skipping work has teeth.
4. **Papers before bullets** — most friction is documents, queues, and reputation—not constant firefights.

## Checkpoints & movement

### Always watched
- **BR — Harahan Bridge** (both directions): full ID + reason for crossing; cargo search; long queues
- Approaches to **HQ-E (Sterick)** and **HQ-W (City Hall)**
- **D1 Wolf Harbor** dock gates (after hours = locked / patrol)
- Levee / riverbank loiter zones near **F1** after dark

### Soft checkpoints (random / reputation-gated)
- CBD grid edges (East)
- Broadway × Missouri core (West)
- Beale / 8th St nightlife districts after 20:00

**Bridge crossing rules (civilian):**
- Needs **Crossing Permit** (day) or mission paperwork
- Occupier-favoring reputation → shorter queue / fewer questions
- Resistance-leaning or “unknown” → secondary screening
- Weapons / unexplained cargo → arrest

**Fiction note:** No other road bridge in playable map (locked). Rail on Harahan/Frisco is NPC/background unless a mission says otherwise; player does not freely ride military rail.

## Identity papers (player)

Everyone carries a **Residence Card** (bank-specific):
- Name, age (18+), address (H1 or H2), occupation, issuing authority (German civil admin East / Japanese civil-military West)

| Document | Who issues | Unlocks |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Residence Card | Local occupation admin | Baseline street presence |
| Work Pass | Employer (W1 factory / W2 farm) + stamp | Daytime work zones; limited curfew exception on shift |
| Crossing Permit | Bridge authority (scarce) | Harahan legal crossing |
| Agency Provisional | Otto (East) / Takeshi (West) | Agency building access; some night moves |
| Resistance Token | Informal (Connor) | Not a legal doc — social passphrase / contact recognition |

**Lost papers:** major friction until replaced (queue + bribe / favor / handler).

## Money & economy

**Status:** Currency **names LOCKED** (2026-09-10); amounts still TBD

### Official currencies (not freely interchangeable at face value)
| Bank | Currency | Short | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|-------|
| **East (German)** | **US Marks** | USM | Occupation issue for German-held Memphis; shops/wages/fines on East bank |
| **West (Japanese)** | **Southern Development Bank notes** | SDB notes | Japanese occupation issue (echoes historical Southern Development Bank / 南方開発金庫 military scrip); West wages, shops, fines |

### Soft / black-market layer
- **Hard goods / USD remnants / silver** — rare cross-bank grease; hangouts (R1–R3), Connor jobs may pay goods not cash

### Combined money rules (LOCKED 2026-11-07, merges hub and core design doc §12)
- **Both currencies are accepted on either bank.** You can pay in US Marks or SDB notes anywhere; exchange is optional, not required to shop.
- **US Marks are worth more than SDB notes at a fixed gap** that holds 1950–55. Prices reflect it. The exact ratio is TBD.
- **Faction shops and exchange desks** give better prices/rates the more that faction likes you. **Civilian markets do not** change with standing.
- Old US dollars still turn up but are **useless as tender** (collector/flavor only).
- Shop signs and tags are in English plus the occupier's language.
- Contraband (arms, munitions) sells only to Resistance fences. Valuables and intel can sell to factions or the Resistance.
- Black-market changers stay gated by Resistance standing (below).

### Exchange (US Marks ↔ SDB notes) — LOCKED principle
**The base gap is fixed (USM > SDB), but there is no clean desk rate.** On top of the fixed gap, what you get depends on **relationship with the controlling faction of the bank where you exchange**.

| Venue | Who | Rate driver |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| **Occupation exchange desk** (near HQ / bridge admin) | German clerks East / Japanese clerks West | Standing with **that bank’s overlord** (German East / Japanese West) |
| **Black-market changer** (hangouts R1–R3, Connor network) | Fixers | Standing with **Resistance** (and risk if high overlord flags you) |

**Working formula (tune numbers later):**
- Each bank has a **base spread** (always worse than “fair”).
- **Overlord reputation** on that bank shifts the desk rate: high → nearer fair; low → punitive / refused.
- Exchanging *into* a bank’s currency is easier if that overlord likes you; exchanging *out* of it while disliked gets worse haircuts.
- **Double agents:** good overlord rate at the desk, but black-market fixers give worse rates or refuse; inverse if resistance-trusted and overlord-cold.
- Failed / refused exchange can flag you for a checkpoint search (especially on Harahan).

| Overlord standing (desk) | Rate quality (design) |
|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Hostile / unknown | Refused or extreme haircut |
| Neutral | Bad but usable |
| Favored (agency path, informants) | Best legal rate |
| Elite / handler-backed | Near-fair + priority queue |

| Resistance standing (black market) | Rate quality |
|------------------------------------|--------------|
| Unknown | No access |
| Trusted contact | Usable underground rate |
| High (come-clean path) | Best underground rate; risk if watched |

Exact % TBD in balance pass — **relationship → rate** is the rule.

| Earn | Amount (design units) | Paid in |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| Farm/factory shift (2B) | Small steady | Local bank currency (USM East / SDB West) |
| Agency stipend (2A) | Medium | Local bank currency; strings attached |
| Side odd jobs | Variable | Often goods or mixed |
| Bribes / fines | Sink | Demanded in local currency |

**Shops:** keep real hours and close at night. They accept either currency; local currency is simplest.  
**Black market:** hangouts after Connor trust; hotter if player is agency-flagged.

## Work life

### East — factory (W1)
- Shift ~07:00–16:00 (or 08:00–17:00)
- Production for Reich logistics (ammo crates / textiles / warehouse — flavor)
- Missing shift without excuse → wage cut + slight overlord reputation drop + supervisor notice (can feed coercion)

### West — farm (W2 / F1 tract)
- Dawn to late afternoon seasonal rhythm; cotton / general crops
- Quotas to occupation buyers
- Missing work → same pressure pattern as East, rural flavor (overseer / Kempeitai visit)

### Agency life (post-2A)
- Report to HQ mornings unless handler says otherwise
- “Cover job” may still exist on papers (farm/factory) for infiltration — **open design choice** (recommend: yes, keeps world coherent)

## What the player can always do (free roam baseline)
- Walk own bank indoors of curfew with papers
- Work shifts when employed
- Shop in open hours
- Visit home / family
- Enter approved hangout
- Sleep / pass time at home (advance clock)

## What needs a mission, pass, or high trust
- Cross Harahan
- Enter HQ freely
- Night movement off home tract
- Enter D1 secure warehouse
- Carry weapons openly
- Meet resistance leadership (Boss / Swims) — not daily life
